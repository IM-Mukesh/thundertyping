TWO THINGS THAT MATTER FOR HOW THESE ARE USED

These sit in front of the card artwork and rise out of it on hover, scaling up
substantially. So:

1. Generate at the LARGEST size available, 2048px or more on the long edge.
   They are displayed small but scale up on interaction, and an undersized
   render goes soft exactly when the player is looking straight at it.

2. Keep clear space around the whole figure. Nothing may touch the frame edge —
   no hair, no boots, no weapon tip, no trailing cape or energy ribbon. Anything
   touching an edge will read as cut off when the character lifts out of the card.
